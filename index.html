CPP Farm Store Omnichannel Consulting Project

A semester-long consulting engagement focused on connecting the CPP Farm Store's physical retail experience with ecommerce, digital product discovery, customer journey improvements, campaign strategy, and performance analytics.

View Final ReportExplore DeliverablesGitHub Repository
Project Overview

One connected retail strategy.

The project evaluates how CPP Farm Store can improve the customer journey across discovery, consideration, purchase, fulfillment, and post-purchase engagement without losing the strengths of its physical campus location.

Consulting Objective

Develop an evidence-based and practical omnichannel strategy that strengthens ecommerce, supports online-to-offline shopping, improves product discoverability, creates higher-value digital experiences such as gift ordering, and gives the Farm Store a repeatable measurement framework.

Core Recommendation

Build the digital experience around a curated Shopify storefront, structured product feeds, coordinated campaigns, gift-basket ordering, and analytics that connect customer behavior to operational and marketing decisions.
